# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Overview
Businesses often struggle to understand customer purchasing patterns, customer loyalty, and the effectiveness of discounts. This project analyzes customer shopping data to uncover actionable insights and help businesses improve marketing strategies, customer retention, and revenue growth.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze customer shopping behavior and purchasing trends.
- Identify top-performing and highly rated products.
- Evaluate discount usage and subscription patterns.
- Segment customers based on purchase history.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for business decision-making.

## 📊 Dataset
- **Records:** 3,900 purchases
- **Features:** 18 columns
- **Data includes:** Customer demographics, products, purchase amounts, discounts, subscriptions, shipping types, and review ratings.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Python:** Pandas, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA)
- **PostgreSQL:** SQL queries and business analysis
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and data visualization
- **Excel/CSV:** Dataset storage and data handling

## 🔄 Project Workflow
1. **Data Loading:** Imported the dataset into Python.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing review ratings and standardized column names.
3. **Feature Engineering:** Created age groups and purchase frequency features.
4. **SQL Analysis:** Analyzed revenue, products, discounts, subscriptions, and customer segments using PostgreSQL.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Built an interactive Power BI dashboard to visualize key metrics.
6. **Business Recommendations:** Developed insights to support better marketing and customer retention decisions.

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights
- Total customers and average purchase amount.
- Average customer review rating.
- Revenue and sales by product category.
- Customer subscription status.
- Revenue and sales by age group.
- Interactive filters for customer demographics, categories, subscriptions, and shipping types.

## 💡 Key Findings
- Analyzed customer purchasing patterns across different product categories.
- Identified products with high discount usage.
- Compared subscriber and non-subscriber purchasing behavior.
- Segmented customers into new, returning, and loyal groups.
- Evaluated revenue contributions across age groups.

## 🚀 Business Impact
The analysis provides actionable insights to help businesses:
- Improve customer loyalty and retention programs.
- Optimize discount and promotional strategies.
- Identify high-performing products.
- Develop targeted marketing campaigns.
- Discover opportunities to improve subscription adoption and revenue.
